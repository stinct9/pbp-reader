**On a low day:** chapters 25 (Sukh-Dukh), 11 (Khap-Sahanshilta), 8 (Het-Prit), 21 (Pragat).
Ch. 25 in particular is direct about hardship — Shriharí names it as something even the
greatest devotees met with, and repeatedly ties it to what it produces rather than treating
it as a sign that something has gone wrong.

**Cross-reading with the Vachanamrut:** the two texts often meet on the same ground. When a
PBP passage answers a question, it's worth grepping `../Vachanamrut/` for the same term — the
Vachanamrut usually gives the fuller argument, PBP the more concentrated statement.
